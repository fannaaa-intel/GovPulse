Privacy Policy

GovPulse · Last updated 3 September 2026

GovPulse is a citizen reporting application for local government. It lets residents submit reports, suggestions and feedback to their local government unit (LGU), and lets LGU staff respond. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can have it deleted.

Information we collect

DataWhy
Name, email address, phone numberTo create your account and let the LGU contact you about your reports.
Government ID image and a selfieIdentity verification only, so that reports come from real residents. Reviewed by authorised LGU staff.
Photos and videos you attachEvidence for the report, suggestion or feedback you submit.
Location (GPS)To place your report on a map so the LGU can find the issue. Captured only when you take a photo in the app or submit a report.
Report, suggestion and feedback contentThe substance of what you send to your LGU, including any community posts and comments.
Device push tokenTo notify you when your report changes status.
Anonymous reports. You may submit a report anonymously. Your identity is then hidden from LGU staff consoles, but the report remains linked to your account so that you can track it. Anonymity hides you from staff; it does not delete your data.

How we use your information

Your information is used to operate the service: to route your submissions to the correct LGU office, to let staff respond, to verify that submissions come from genuine residents, and to notify you of progress. Report content may be processed by an automated system to categorise it and route it to the right department.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

Authorised staff of your LGU can see the submissions routed to them. Community posts and comments you choose to publish are visible to other users of the app. Your government ID and selfie are restricted to verification review and are not visible to other residents.

We use service providers to run the app — Supabase for our database and file storage, and Google Firebase for push notifications. They process data on our behalf under their own security obligations.

Facebook Login

If you sign in with Facebook, we receive your name and email address from Facebook to create your account. We do not post to your Facebook account and do not access your friends list. You can revoke this at any time in your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Submitted reports are kept as part of the LGU's public service record. Identity verification images are retained while your account is verified, and are deleted when your account is deleted.

Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data. See the Data Deletion page for how to do this.

This service is operated in the Philippines and handles personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Children

GovPulse is intended for residents aged 18 and over, as identity verification requires a government-issued ID. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
