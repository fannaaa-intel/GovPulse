Delete Your Data

GovPulse · Last updated 3 September 2026

You can ask us to delete your GovPulse account and the personal information associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to reducamark7@gmail.com from the email address registered to your GovPulse account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my GovPulse account.
  3. We will confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days, and email you when it is done.
Sending the request from your registered email address is how we confirm the request is genuinely yours. If you no longer have access to that address, tell us in your message and we will verify your identity another way.

Why some report content is kept

Reports you submitted form part of your local government's service record — they may document work that was carried out, or issues still being resolved. When your account is deleted, these reports are anonymised: your name and contact details are removed, so the report remains as a record of the issue but is no longer linked to you.

If you want a specific report removed entirely, say so in your email and we will consider it alongside your LGU's record-keeping obligations.

Deleting Facebook Login access

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also disconnect GovPulse from your Facebook account: Facebook → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → GovPulse → Remove. This stops Facebook sharing data with us, but does not delete your GovPulse account — use the email request above for that.
